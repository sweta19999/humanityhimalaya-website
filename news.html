Pittsburgh Community Event · October 25, 2026

Coming Together for Nepal

A Benefit Concert of Music, Culture, and Compassion for Nepal Flood Relief

October 22, 2026 · Event Announcement

As Nepal continues to face the devastating effects of flooding, the Pittsburgh community is invited to come together for an evening of music, Nepalese culture, and support for families affected by the disaster.

Date Sunday, October 25, 2026
Time 6:00 PM–8:30 PM
Venue Bellefield Hall, University of Pittsburgh
Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA
Admission Free for students and children under 17
$15 cash for non-students on arrival

Additional on-site donations are welcome. Except for minimal hall-related technical expenses, all concert proceeds will be sent directly toward serving victims in Nepal to support disaster-relief efforts.

An Evening of Music and Nepalese Culture

The program brings together musicians and singers from the University of Pittsburgh, Carnegie Mellon University, Chatham University, and the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra, alongside Nepalese cultural dances and songs performed by members of the community.

The musical program includes piano, violin and other string performances, classical vocal selections, and works by composers including Mozart, Chopin, Rachmaninoff, Bach, and Villa-Lobos. The program may include additional performers and selections.

Come Together in Support of Nepal

This community gathering offers an opportunity for Nepalese and non-Nepalese residents, students, families, and friends of Nepal to show solidarity with people affected by the floods. Through music and culture, the evening aims to bring people together in compassion and support for relief efforts.

Have Questions? We’re happy to help! If you have any questions or would like more information about the "Coming Together for Nepal" event, donations, our Nepal flood relief efforts, or how to get involved, please contact us. We’d be happy to provide additional information. We appreciate your interest and support for Humanity Himalaya!

August 26, 2026

Catastrophic Flash Flood Devastates Communities in Nepal

Nepal was struck by a catastrophic flash flood on August 26, 2026, following an ice-and-rock avalanche and glacier-related slope failure in the Himalayan border region.

The resulting surge of water, mud, rock and debris travelled downstream through the affected river systems, causing widespread destruction across communities.

Homes, roads, bridges, hydropower facilities and other critical infrastructure have been severely damaged or destroyed. Entire communities have been displaced, while families continue to search for loved ones who remain missing.

Nepal is facing an extremely serious humanitarian crisis. Entire communities have been devastated, and affected families urgently need humanitarian assistance as rescue, recovery and relief operations continue.

Search-and-rescue teams and government agencies continue working to reach affected communities, recover victims, evacuate survivors and provide emergency assistance.

The scale of the destruction has created enormous challenges for families who have lost homes, livelihoods, transportation routes and access to essential services.

Humanity Himalaya's Commitment

Humanity Himalaya stands with the people and communities affected by this devastating disaster.

We are committed to supporting humanitarian relief, recovery and rebuilding efforts where assistance is most needed, with a focus on vulnerable families and communities affected by the disaster.
